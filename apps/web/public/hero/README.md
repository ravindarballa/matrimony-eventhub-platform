# Hero imagery

`wedding-mandap.jpg` is the backdrop behind the heading on the landing page, the
browse pages and the enquiry page. Replacing it is dropping a file in here with
the same name and reloading — nothing in the code names anything but the path
`/hero/wedding-mandap.jpg`.

## What is here now

A Hindu wedding under a floral mandap, with a red floral **Ganesha** motif on the
backdrop behind the couple — the deity invoked at the start of a ceremony, and
the one that actually appears in mandap decoration.

- **Source:** [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/wedding-ceremony-under-floral-canopy-7O422yG_b80)
- **Photographer:** Amish Thakkar
- **Licence:** the Unsplash Licence — free to use commercially, no attribution
  required. The credit above is courtesy, not obligation.

It is a placeholder. It shows real people at their own wedding, which is fine
under that licence but is not your photography, so replace it before this goes
in front of anyone.

## What to replace it with

The frame is cropped to roughly 3:1 on a wide screen and the heading sits over
the left half on the landing page, centred elsewhere. That means:

- **Wide and calm on the left.** A busy subject on the left fights the headline.
  The current photo works because the mandap is centred and the sky is empty.
- **2000px wide or more.** It is displayed full-bleed, so anything smaller goes
  soft on a large monitor.
- **Mid-tone, not blown out.** A scrim darkens it for legibility; a very bright
  photo needs a heavier scrim, and a heavy scrim is what made the earlier
  attempts look muddy.

If you want a Venkateshwara mandap specifically, that is worth sourcing from a
photographer or a licensed Indian stock library — free stock does not carry it.
Drop it in here under the same name and it appears everywhere at once.
